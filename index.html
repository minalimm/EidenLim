EIDEN

Raised between California and Tokyo. Currently studying Dramatic Writing at NYU Tisch School of the Arts.

EIDEN (CONT'D)

(re: the work)

Stories are how we organize reality. Going into one feels like splitting the atom — the discovery of a deeper, denser layer informing what we do and why.

EIDEN (CONT'D)

My characters are drawn to act because of stories, so I write toward the collision: people trying to prove their existence makes sense inside the larger stories around them.
